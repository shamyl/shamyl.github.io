**DARPA Heavy Lift Challenge: Conventional Helicopter Wins at 3.84:1**
Avidrone Inc. won the DARPA Lift Challenge with the Katana, a conventional single-main-rotor helicopter achieving a 3.84:1 payload-to-weight ratio — hauling 51 kg (112.4 lbs) of cast iron on a 13.3 kg (29.3 lbs) aircraft. The result is nearly four times better than any production heavy-lift drone. Notably, the winning design was the least exotic machine in the competition: a traditional helicopter configuration that outperformed multirotors and experimental layouts. Since no team reached the 4:1 target, prize money was halved — Avidrone collected $1.25 million instead of $2.5 million. The key engineering finding: lift wasn't the bottleneck; energy storage was, with multiple teams running out of battery power mid-course.
