Keynote at FIC 2026, organized by LearnOBots. The talk explores how to deliver future-ready learning and what LearnOBots has built to support it: curriculum, the LearnOSTEAM platform, makerspaces, FutureLabs, hardware kits, and teacher guidance. Covers skills for the AI era, the educational shift toward connected disciplines and active learning, and the impact across students, teachers, and schools.
